OUR STORY

We make clothes we actually want to wear.

RangLoop is a homegrown Indian clothing brand started by Khushi Kesarwani and Abhirab Datrye. Two unserious people who decided to do something seriously this time — design clothes that actually mean something. Every design on this site was made by us.

We started with zero. No investment, no factory, no plan B. Just designs, a lot of chai, and the belief that your wardrobe deserves something original.
